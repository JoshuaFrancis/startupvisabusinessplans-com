What's included

Four components in every Start-Up Visa business plan.

01

Executive summary

A two-page summary written to the standard IRCC reviewers and designated organization intake teams apply. Covers the innovation thesis, market opportunity, team qualifications, and the specific benefit to Canada's economy.

Innovation and IP narrative

A structured section demonstrating that the venture meets the innovation threshold required for SUV eligibility: novel technology or process, defensible differentiation, and a clear IP ownership and protection strategy.

Financial model

Five-year income statement, cash flow projection, and balance sheet built on documented assumptions. Includes use-of-funds detail aligned with the specific investment or support commitment from the designated organization.
